FIND THE EVIDENCE

搜尋與篩選

顯示全部 69 筆文獻。

DIRECT T1D

研究族群直接包含 T1D；仍需看設計、樣本、BMI 與 AID 使用情境。

INDIRECT

來自 T2D、obesity 或一般 perioperative evidence，只能支援機轉或風險管理。

GUIDANCE

共識、guideline、label 或方法學資源，不等同 randomized efficacy evidence。

01 · FOUNDATION

核心共識、standards 與 guideline context

    回答：Weekly semaglutide／tirzepatide 如何整合 insulin、CGM、ketone、GI、eye、nutrition、pregnancy、procedure 與 access。

    界線：Consensus safe-use framework；不是 regulatory approval 或 phase 3 outcome proof。

02 · DIRECT EFFICACY

Randomized trials 與 systematic reviews

評讀時優先看 randomization、BMI、AID 使用、duration、insulin protocol 與 ketosis ascertainment。

  1. RCT · T1D原文 ref 63

    Mathieu C, Zinman B, Hemmingsson JU, et al. Efficacy and Safety of Liraglutide Added to Insulin Treatment in T1D: ADJUNCT ONE.

    Diabetes Care. 2016;39:1702–1710. DOI: 10.2337/dc16-0691.

    回答：52-week、n=1,398 的 HbA1c、weight、insulin 與 dose-related safety trade-off。

    界線：Daily liraglutide 不能等同 weekly semaglutide 或 dual GIP/GLP-1 agonism。

    PubMedDOI
  2. RCT · T1D原文 ref 64

    Ahrén B, Hirsch IB, Pieber TR, et al. Liraglutide Added to Capped Insulin Treatment: ADJUNCT TWO.

    Diabetes Care. 2016;39:1693–1701. DOI: 10.2337/dc16-0690.

    回答：26-week、n=835，預設 insulin cap 下 efficacy 與 hypoglycemia／ketosis。

    界線：證明 insulin protocol 會改變 benefit-risk，但未消除 ketosis concern。

    PubMedDOI
  3. RCT · NEW-ONSET T1D原文 ref 37

    Dejgaard TF, Frandsen CS, Kielgast U, et al. Liraglutide Enhances Insulin Secretion and Prolongs the Remission Period in Newly Diagnosed T1D: NewLira.

    Diabetes Obes Metab. 2024;26:4905–4915. DOI: 10.1111/dom.15889.

    回答：Residual C-peptide phenotype 的 stimulated C-peptide、insulin requirement 與 remission。

    界線：不能外推 long-standing、C-peptide-negative T1D。

    PubMedDOI
  4. CROSSOVER RCT · T1D原文 ref 48

    Pasqua M-R, Tsoukas MA, Kobayati A, et al. Subcutaneous Weekly Semaglutide with Automated Insulin Delivery in T1D.

    Nature Medicine. 2025;31:1239–1245. DOI: 10.1038/s41591-024-03463-z.

    回答：Semaglutide + AID 對 TIR 的 direct randomized evidence；亦記錄 recurrent euglycemic ketosis without acidosis。

    界線：28 randomized、24 completed；短期且不足以排除罕見 harm。

    Open full textPubMed
  5. RCT · T1D + OBESITY原文 ref 21

    Shah VN, Akturk HK, Kruger D, et al. Semaglutide in Adults with Type 1 Diabetes and Obesity: ADJUST-T1D.

    NEJM Evidence. 2025;4:EVIDoa2500173. DOI: 10.1056/EVIDoa2500173.

    回答：26-week、n=72、皆使用 AID；composite glycemia/weight outcome 與 adverse events。

    界線：BMI ≥30、high-technology cohort；不能直接外推 normal BMI 或未用 AID。

    DOI / publisher
  6. PHASE 2 RCT · T1D原文 ref 52

    Snaith JR, Frampton R, Samocha-Bonet D, et al. Tirzepatide in Adults With Type 1 Diabetes: A Phase 2 Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial.

    Diabetes Care. 2026;49:161–170. DOI: 10.2337/dc25-2379.

    回答：12-week tirzepatide 2.5→5 mg 對 weight、HbA1c、TDD 與短期 tolerability。

    界線：n=24、BMI >30；效果估計不穩定，不能回答 long-term DKA 或 cardiorenal outcomes。

    Open full textPubMed
  7. SYSTEMATIC REVIEW · T1D2026

    Purcell A, Zhen XM, Wong J, Glastras SJ. GLP-1 RA Treatment Reduces Body Weight and Improves Glycaemic Outcomes in T1D with Overweight/Obesity.

    Diabetes Obes Metab. 2026;28:296–305. DOI: 10.1111/dom.70188.

    回答：13 studies 的 pooled weight、HbA1c、TDD、hypoglycemia 與 GI effects。

    界線：Older agents、designs 與 insulin protocols 異質；pooled class effect 不能當 molecule-specific estimate。

    Open full textPubMed
  8. META-ANALYSIS · T1D原文 ref 69

    Tan X, Pan X, Wu X, et al. GLP-1 Receptor Agonists as Add-on Therapy to Insulin for Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus.

    Front Pharmacol. 2023;14:975880. DOI: 10.3389/fphar.2023.975880.

    回答：Pre-new-generation evidence 的 HbA1c、weight、insulin dose 與 adverse events synthesis。

    界線：主要受 liraglutide/exenatide data 主導，不能代表 tirzepatide。

    Open full text

03 · REAL-WORLD EVIDENCE

Semaglutide／tirzepatide 長期與 clinical-practice signals

這一組最貼近日常照護，也最容易受到 selection、channeling、attrition 與 adverse-event undercapture 影響。

  1. OBSERVATIONAL · T1D原文 ref 22

    Snell-Bergeon JK, Kaur G, Renner D, et al. Effectiveness of Semaglutide and Tirzepatide in Overweight and Obese Adults with T1D.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2025;27:1–9. DOI: 10.1089/dia.2024.0328.

    回答：兩個 agent 在 12 個月的 weight 與 HbA1c real-world change。

    界線：非 head-to-head randomization；跨 cohort 差異不能證明 tirzepatide superiority。

    DOI / publisher
  2. MATCHED RETROSPECTIVE · T1D原文 ref 44

    Garg SK, Kaur G, Haider Z, et al. Efficacy of Semaglutide in Overweight and Obese Patients with Type 1 Diabetes.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2024;26:184–189. DOI: 10.1089/dia.2023.0490.

    回答：50 treated vs 50 matched controls、1-year weight/BMI 與 CGM signals。

    界線：Retrospective、mostly White，且 baseline insulin/HbA1c 並非完全平衡。

    PubMedDOI
  3. RETROSPECTIVE · T1D原文 ref 23

    Garg SK, Akturk HK, Kaur G, et al. Efficacy and Safety of Tirzepatide in Overweight and Obese Adult Patients with T1D.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2024;26:367–374. DOI: 10.1089/dia.2024.0050.

    回答：Early tirzepatide real-world weight、glycemia、insulin 與 tolerability signal。

    界線：Observational proof-of-concept；處方 selection 與 missing follow-up 可放大效果。

    DOI / publisher
  4. 21-MONTH COHORT · T1D原文 ref 41

    Garg SK, Kaur G, Renner D, et al. Cardiovascular and Renal Biomarkers in Overweight and Obese Adults with T1D Treated with Tirzepatide for 21 Months.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2025;27:152–160. DOI: 10.1089/dia.2024.0481.

    回答：較長期 weight、HbA1c 與 biomarker trajectory。

    界線：Biomarker improvement 不能替代 randomized MACE／kidney outcomes。

    DOI / publisher
  5. REAL-WORLD · T1D原文 ref 43

    Al Ozairi E, Irshad M, Alkandari J, et al. Weight Loss in People with T1D over 12 Months: Tirzepatide, Semaglutide and Liraglutide.

    Diabetes Obes Metab. 2026;28:166–173. DOI: 10.1111/dom.70172.

    回答：同一 real-world setting 中三種 incretin regimens 的 12-month patterns。

    界線：Nonrandom treatment allocation，不能直接作 comparative efficacy ranking。

    DOI / publisher
  6. REAL-WORLD · T1D原文 ref 45

    Mertens J, De Winter HT, Dirinck E, et al. Adjunctive Semaglutide, Weight, Glycemic Control and MASLD in People with T1D.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2026;28:27–37. DOI: 10.1177/15209156251362497.

    回答：12-month weight、HbA1c、TIR 與 metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease variables。

    界線：TIR change 未顯著；real-world hepatic measures 需注意 ascertainment。

    DOI / publisher
  7. 2-YEAR REAL-WORLD · T1D原文 ref 46

    Al Hayek AA, Klonoff DC, Al Zahrani WM, et al. Semaglutide Add-on Therapy and CGM Outcomes in Adults with T1D: Two-Year Real-World Data.

    J Diabetes Complications. 2025;39:109064. DOI: 10.1016/j.jdiacomp.2025.109064.

    回答：目前較長期 semaglutide T1D glucose、TIR、weight trajectory。

    界線：無 randomized comparator；retention 與 concurrent care changes 可能影響結果。

    DOI / publisher
  8. CHART REVIEW · T1D原文 ref 47

    Almohareb SN, Alfayez OM, Aljuaid SS, et al. Effectiveness and Safety of GLP-1 Receptor Agonists in Patients with Type 1 Diabetes.

    J Clin Med. 2024;13:6532. DOI: 10.3390/jcm13216532.

    回答：較大 chart review 的 HbA1c、weight、TIR 與 safety experience。

    界線：Semaglutide／liraglutide 混合，且 TIR 可用資料與追蹤時點不完整。

    Open full text
  9. OBSERVATIONAL · T1D原文 ref 51

    Gutierrez RR, Tama E, Bechenati D, et al. Effect of Tirzepatide on Body Weight and Diabetes Control in Adults with T1D and Overweight or Obesity.

    Mayo Clin Proc. 2025;100:265–275. DOI: 10.1016/j.mayocp.2024.07.006.

    回答：Clinical-practice weight、HbA1c 與 insulin dose change。

    界線：n=51、96% White，12-month subset 更小；generalizability 有限。

    PubMedDOI
  10. TARGET-TRIAL EMULATION · T1D原文 ref 57

    Xu Y, Malek ND, Chang AR, et al. GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Major Cardiovascular and Kidney Outcomes in Type 1 Diabetes.

    Nature Medicine. 2026. DOI: 10.1038/s41591-026-04274-0.

    界線：Target-trial emulation 仍受 residual confounding、coding、immortal-time 與 prescribing selection 影響。

    Open full textDOI
  11. DATABASE STUDY · T1D原文 ref 56

    Garg S, Kim S, Ford A, et al. All-Cause Mortality and Health Care Resource Utilization in T1D Treated with GLP-1 RA／GIP.

    Diabetes Technol Ther. 2026;28:271–278. DOI: 10.1177/15209156251403555.

    界線：Database endpoint quality 與 confounding by access/health behavior；不能宣稱 causal reduction。

  12. REAL-WORLD · T1D原文 ref 49

    Cohen N, Yeung A, Jenkins A. Real-World Experience of Adjunct Weekly Semaglutide in Type 1 Diabetes: Is It Worth It?

    Diabetes Res Clin Pract. 2025;219:111979. DOI: 10.1016/j.diabres.2024.111979.

    回答：Routine-care effectiveness、continuation 與 practical tolerability。

    界線：Real-world benefit-risk 受 local prescribing、follow-up 與 discontinuation patterns影響。

    DOI / publisher

04 · INSULIN, AID & KETONES

調整 insulin 與避免 euglycemic ketosis 的實作證據

這組文獻最接近處方後第一週的臨床行動；case reports 只能顯示可能性，不能估 incidence。

  1. T1D + CONTROL-IQ原文 ref 53

    Karakus KE, Klein MP, Akturk HK, et al. Changes in Basal and Bolus Insulin Requirements with Tirzepatide in Adults with T1D Using Tandem Control-IQ.

    Diabetes Ther. 2024;15:1647–1655. DOI: 10.1007/s13300-024-01592-9.

    回答：Prandial 與 basal insulin 下降的時序及 AID parameter implications。

    界線：Specific algorithm、small observational cohort；不可直接套用所有 pumps。

    Open full text
  2. POST-HOC RCT · T1D原文 ref 54

    Pasqua M-R, Tsoukas MA, Haidar A. Changes to Insulin Requirements over Time with Semaglutide in Adults with T1D on Pump Therapy.

    Diabetes Obes Metab. 2026;28:427–433. DOI: 10.1111/dom.70213.

    回答：Bolus/TDD day 7 即下降、basal later decline，以及 ICR/correction factor changes。

    界線：Post-hoc、n=26；提供 titration timing 而非 universal percent-reduction order。

    Open full textPubMed
  3. POST-HOC RCT · T1D原文 ref 68

    Karakus KE, Akturk HK, Kruger D, et al. Semaglutide and Insulin Dose Reduction in Adults with T1D and Obesity Using AID: ADJUST-T1D Post Hoc.

    Diabetes Care. 2026;49:718–723. DOI: 10.2337/dc25-2249.

    回答：AID cohort 的 basal/bolus/TDD adaptation under semaglutide。

    界線：Exploratory analysis；原 trial 不是以 insulin-component trajectory 為 primary outcome。

    DOI / publisher
  4. PRACTICAL GUIDANCE原文 ref 70

    Saeed ZI, Akturk HK, Aleppo G, et al. Insulin Titration Recommendations When Using GLP-1 RA Therapy in Adults with T1D.

    Clinical Diabetes. 2025;43:131–138. DOI: 10.2337/cd24-0067.

    回答：依 baseline HbA1c/CGM 與 hypo burden 設計的 starting reductions 與 follow-up。

    界線：Clinical recommendations，並非比較不同 reduction algorithms 的 randomized trial。

    Open full textPubMed
  5. QUALITATIVE · T1D原文 ref 79

    Pasqua M-R, Doumat J, Tsoukas MA, et al. Semaglutide with AID in Adults with T1D: Qualitative Analyses and Patient-Reported Outcomes.

    Can J Diabetes. 2026;50:38–46.e5. DOI: 10.1016/j.jcjd.2025.10.175.

    回答：Nausea、fear of vomiting、carbohydrate estimation 與 bolus decision 的 lived impact。

    界線：Small nested qualitative sample；可解釋 mechanism，不估 event frequency。

    DOI / publisher
  6. TECHNOLOGY GUIDANCE原文 ref 76

    Dhatariya K, Bergenstal RM, Al-Sofiani M, et al. Continuous Ketone Monitoring for People with Diabetes: International Expert Recommendations.

    Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol. 2026;14:82–92. DOI: 10.1016/S2213-8587(25)00331-6.

    回答：Continuous ketone sensing 的 use cases、interpretation 與 research needs。

    界線：Emerging technology；尚不能取代現行 capillary β-hydroxybutyrate sick-day standard。

    DOI / publisher
  7. CASE REPORT · T1D原文 ref 82

    Widhalm C, Pulido V. Euglycemic Diabetic Ketoacidosis in a Patient with T1D after GLP-1 Administration.

    J Investig Med High Impact Case Rep. 2023;11. DOI: 10.1177/23247096231206333.

07 · NUTRITION & LEAN MASS

Body composition、resistance training 與 disordered eating

多數 evidence 來自 obesity/T2D；兩個 T1D liraglutide studies 的 lean-mass 結果不一致。

08 · MEDICATIONS & PREGNANCY

09 · PERIOPERATIVE

Residual gastric content、aspiration 與 hold/continue decision

Case reports 建立 plausibility；cohort/RCT 評估 residual content；guidance 整合 procedure、symptoms、fasting 與 glycemic risk。

  1. CASE REPORT原文 ref 145

    回答：Aspiration concern 的 clinical plausibility。

    界線：Single case；不能估 population risk 或決定 universal withholding interval。
